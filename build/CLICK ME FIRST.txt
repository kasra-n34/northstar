NORTHSTAR — Personal Development OS
=====================================

Northstar is a personal productivity app for tracking your growth across
life pillars: career, health, relationships, finance, and more.


INSTALLATION
------------
1. Drag Northstar to the Applications folder
2. Double-click "Install Northstar" to finish setup
   (This removes a Mac security restriction for unsigned apps)
3. Open Northstar from your Applications folder


REQUIREMENTS
------------
• Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4)
• macOS 12 or later
